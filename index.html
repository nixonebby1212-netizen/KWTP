WHO WE ARE

Empowering Communities. Creating Opportunities.

Kingdom Wealth Transfer Program (KWTP) is a cooperative society established to promote economic empowerment, self-reliance and sustainable community development.

Through opportunity, education, mentorship, financial literacy, entrepreneurship, apprenticeship, innovation and leadership development, KWTP brings people together to build skills, unlock potential and create meaningful opportunities.
